Presented **Show-Ready Humanoids: Interactive and Customizable Robot Performers for
Events and Entertainment** at Innovation Day 2026, hosted by the University of Akron
on October 29. I represented the Advanced Telerobotics Research Lab at Kent State
University with our Booster K1.

The poster brings together our work on video-to-robot motion, a library of motions
tracked by SONIC-K1, and robot customization through clothing, footwear and accessories.
The poster authors are Kyrylo Kolesnichenko, Lucky Sah, Irvin Steve Cardenas and Jong-Hoon Kim.
