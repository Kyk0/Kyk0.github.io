I designed 12 types of shoes for the Booster K1 for the
[Humanoid Robot Fashion Challenge](/blog/robot-fashion-challenge/), where our team won Best
Humanoid Wearable/Accessory. The two we printed are now on
[GitHub](https://github.com/Kyk0/k1-klompen):

- print-ready STLs for both feet, and the script that builds them;
- an ornate version with a heel rosette, tulips on the sides and a rope border;
- the light open-work pair with vines, plus two heavier vine versions;
- the K1 wearing the clogs in MuJoCo and as a URDF, with renders.

A dance policy trained barefoot also runs in the clogs in simulation without falling. Designs
are CC BY 4.0, scripts MIT.
