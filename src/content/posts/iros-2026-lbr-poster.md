*Learning Expressive Humanoid Locomotion from Monocular Runway Videos for Robot Fashion
Shows*, with Irvin Steve Cardenas and Jong-Hoon Kim, in the Late Breaking Results session
of IROS 2026.

From one phone video of a runway walk to a catwalk gait on the real Booster K1: 3D human
motion from the video, retargeting to the K1, a tracking policy trained in simulation, then
the robot. More on the [project page](/projects/expressive-motion/).
