*Can Structured Prompting Overcome Knowledge Bias in Vision-Language Models?*, with Martas
Ambraziunas, at the 31st International Conference on Information Society and University
Studies. Four open models, five prompting strategies: prompting alone does not reliably
reduce the bias.

The paper was published on November 6, 2026 in [CEUR Workshop Proceedings, Vol. 4317](https://ceur-ws.org/Vol-4317/). [Read the published paper](https://ceur-ws.org/Vol-4317/paper19.pdf).
