I’m a final-year undergraduate at Vilnius University and a researcher at Kent State
University’s Advanced Telerobotics Research Lab, advised by Jong-Hoon Kim.

My main interests are reinforcement learning and humanoid robotics. I develop and test
control policies in simulation and on hardware, working on expressive motion,
[locomotion for teleoperation](/projects/k1-loco-teleop/), and
[walking with different foot geometries](/projects/k1-heel-walking/).
I also work on [bipedal running](/projects/) and
[LLM-based human–robot interaction](/projects/mi-rol/).
