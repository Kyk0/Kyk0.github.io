The dances come from the [AIST Dance Video Database](https://aistdancedb.ongaaccel.jp/) by AIST, through the
[AIST++](https://google.github.io/aistplusplus_dataset/) 3D annotations. I retargeted all 1,363 usable clips to the
29-DoF Unitree G1 with PhySINK from PHUMA, chosen in a bake-off against GMR and UMR, and ran each one through NVIDIA's
released SONIC controller in MuJoCo without fine-tuning. The 893 clips it tracks are the dataset: 10 styles, about
2.8 hours, NPZ joint trajectories at 60 fps. All 893 can be played in 3D in the browser in the [viewer](https://kyry.me/g1-dances/viewer/).

Everything is simulation only. With a 30 ms action delay, success on held-out dancers dropped from 64.7% to 17.3%,
so latency robustness is still open before this goes on a real robot.

If you use the data, you must cite Tsuchida et al., *AIST Dance Video Database*, ISMIR 2019. Commercial use is
subject to the [AIST Dance DB Terms of Use](https://aistdancedb.ongaaccel.jp/terms_of_use/).
