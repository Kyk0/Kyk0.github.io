## Summary

Shoes I designed and 3D printed for the Booster K1, in two open-source sets, each in its own
repo:

- **Heels** for runway walks. The 50 mm pair is printed and on the robot.
  [Files](https://github.com/Kyk0/k1-heel-shoes).
- **Clogs and vines** for the
  [Humanoid Robot Fashion Challenge](/blog/robot-fashion-challenge/) at Kent State, where our
  team won Best Humanoid Wearable/Accessory. [Files](https://github.com/Kyk0/k1-klompen).

## Heels

I also have 20, 30 and 40 mm versions of the same shoe, not printed yet.

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Heel | 50 mm |
| Sole pitch | ~15.6° toe down |
| Sole below the bare foot | 58.5 mm |
| Weight | ~350 g per shoe |
| Mounting | three screws through the posts in the footbed |

The [files](https://github.com/Kyk0/k1-heel-shoes) have STEP and STL for both shoes, sim
meshes, and MuJoCo / URDF models of the K1 wearing them. Walking in them:
[K1 Heel Walking](/projects/k1-heel-walking/).

## Clogs and vines

For the challenge I designed 12 types of shoes for the K1. Two were printed for the show, and
both are here: solid Dutch clogs and a light open-work pair with vines. Each prints in one
piece and fits over the K1's foot.

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Size | 229 × 108 × 67 mm |
| Weight | ~350 g per shoe (estimate) |
| Robot height | +2.2 cm |

A dance policy trained barefoot also runs in the clogs in simulation without falling: body
tracking error 4.94 cm, against 4.86 cm barefoot.

The [files](https://github.com/Kyk0/k1-klompen) have the print STLs, the script that builds
the clogs, an ornate version with a heel rosette, side tulips and a rope border, three vine
versions (open-work, relief, full), and the K1 wearing them in MuJoCo and as a URDF.
