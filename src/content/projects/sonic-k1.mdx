## Summary

[SONIC](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.07820) is NVIDIA's whole-body motion tracker for the
Unitree G1: one policy that follows thousands of human motions. SONIC-K1 ports it to the
Booster K1 and trains it from scratch on about 10,000 Bones-SEED mocap clips retargeted to
the K1, in about 100 GPU-hours on a shared H100, about 1% of SONIC's training compute. v1.0 adds
a short post-training for the real robot (Booster's torque limits and 0-40 ms of actuation delay,
4,000 more iterations). Code, weights, the robot app and the K1 USD are public; details in the
[preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.39327).

## Results (simulation)

Success on motions the policy never trained on, with the SONIC paper's own success rule, in
MuJoCo:

| benchmark | SONIC-K1 (K1) | SONIC (G1)* | BeyondMimic (G1)* | Any2Track (G1)* |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| PHUMA, external benchmark | 92.3% | 97.2% | 73.8% | 78.5% |
| held-out mocap (ours: 1,985 Bones-SEED clips) | 97.4% | 98.5% | 82.0% | 61.6% |
| held-out, real-robot torque limits + 40 ms delay | 96.6% (v0.1: 76.7%) | | | |

\* As reported in the SONIC paper, on the Unitree G1 with their own data and retargeting: an
indicative comparison, not a controlled one.

## How

- SONIC's training code, adapted to the K1's 22 joints, with motions retargeted from human
  mocap to the K1 with [UMR](https://github.com/hanyang9/UMR).
- Trained headless in Isaac Lab on a shared H100, following my
  [Isaac Lab on H100 guide](https://kyry.me/isaaclab-h100/).
- Exported to ONNX: the policy runs on a laptop in MuJoCo, no GPU and no Isaac needed
  (quick start on the [project page](https://kyry.me/sonic-k1/)).

v1.0 runs on the real K1 with the open [robot app](https://github.com/Kyk0/sonic-k1/tree/main/sonic_k1_deploy);
in our runs it walks and turns without the leg shaking of the first release (see the preprint).
Next: hardware evaluation, whole-body teleoperation from a VR headset, and video to K1. Demo motions for it: [K1 Moves](https://huggingface.co/datasets/Kyry10/k1-moves).

Training data includes Motion Data by [Bones Studio](https://bones.studio/); use of the
underlying dataset is subject to the BONES Motion Capture Dataset License Agreement. Weights
are released for non-commercial research (CC BY-NC 4.0). Built on SONIC by NVIDIA GEAR (code
Apache-2.0); robot model by Booster Robotics (BSD-3).
