## Summary

[Microduck Arena](https://huggingface.co/spaces/pollen-robotics/microduck-arena) is Pollen
Robotics' leaderboard for their small bipedal robot: a 2 m sprint, a 1.5 m climb up an 8°
slope and a 2 m sprint over passive rollers, each scored as the mean finish time over five
seeds. I started from a licensed running policy (Hannes von Essen's
[microduck-running](https://huggingface.co/HannesVonEssen/microduck-running), Apache-2.0)
and fine-tuned it for each course with PPO.

| course | best mean (5/5 finishes) | published entry |
|---|---|---|
| 2 m sprint | 1.084 s | 1.096 s |
| 8° hill | 1.360 s | 1.360 s |
| passive rollers | 1.516 s | 1.648 s, #1 on the leaderboard |

Simulation only (MuJoCo). The 1.516 s roller policy is newer than the published entry and
was not yet submitted.

## How

- Kept the policy interface fixed (61 observations, 14 actions, the original normalizer), so
  every checkpoint can be scored by the Arena as is.
- Trained on the race itself: the real lane, slope or rollers, start from standing, narrow
  start and actuator-delay noise; reward for forward progress, finish bonus, fall penalty.
- Every 100 training iterations the checkpoint was exported to ONNX and scored by the Arena
  on five seeds. Only policies that finish all five count; the training reward was never
  used to pick one.
- One change per experiment. A lower learning rate on a full PPO resume took the roller gait
  from 1.648 s to 1.516 s. On the sprint, static gait trims and longer runs did not beat the
  selected policy. The fastest flat-ground policy, run as is, finished only 1 of 5 seeds on the
  hill and none on the rollers: each course needed its own fine-tuning.

Each experiment, including the ones that failed, is in the
[repository on Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/Kyry10/microduck-arena-lab/blob/main/docs/EXPERIMENTS.md),
with the scripts, per-seed scores and the weights.
