The companion of [G1 Dances](/projects/g1-dances/) for the Booster K1. The dances come from the
[AIST Dance Video Database](https://aistdancedb.ongaaccel.jp/) by AIST, through the
[AIST++](https://google.github.io/aistplusplus_dataset/) 3D annotations. I retargeted all 1,363 usable clips to the
22-DoF K1 with [UMR](https://github.com/hanyang9/UMR) and ran each one through [SONIC-K1](/projects/sonic-k1/) v1.0 in
MuJoCo without fine-tuning. The 1046 clips it tracks (76.7%) are the dataset: 10 styles, about 3.1 hours, NPZ
joint trajectories at 60 fps. All of them can be played in 3D in the browser in the [viewer](https://kyry.me/k1-dances/viewer/).

Everything is simulation only, and tracking was checked without action delay. SONIC-K1 itself runs on a real K1,
but these dances have not been run on one yet.

If you use the data, you must cite Tsuchida et al., *AIST Dance Video Database*, ISMIR 2019. Commercial use is
subject to the [AIST Dance DB Terms of Use](https://aistdancedb.ongaaccel.jp/terms_of_use/).
