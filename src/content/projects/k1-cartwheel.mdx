## Summary

A cartwheel is the hardest move I've tried on the K1. The policy does it in simulation,
but on the real robot it hasn't finished one yet: the hand doesn't take the weight and
the robot goes down.

## How

The motion comes from a cartwheel video, through GVHMR and GMR to the K1. I trained it
with BeyondMimic in Isaac Lab, first as is, then reworked it for the robot: only inputs
the robot can really measure, a standing start and a standing finish, the head held at
zero, and penalties for running the motors and joints at their limits. In MuJoCo the
final policy goes from standing to a cartwheel and back.

## On the robot

It falls in the middle of the move. The motors on the robot pulled up to about 2.7× the
torque the sim allows, because the policy leans on motor limits that aren't the same on
the robot. During the fast rotation the IMU orientation also drifts far from what the
gyro says, and the policy reacts strongly to it.

Next: match the motor limits between training, sim and robot, make the policy robust to
the orientation input, and check that the hand and head clear the floor, then train again.
